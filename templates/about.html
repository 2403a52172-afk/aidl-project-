Comprehensive design, theoretical methodology, and empirical evaluation for deepfake speech detection.

1. Problem Statement & Motivation

With the rapid democratization of deep generative models, such as neural vocoders (WaveNet, HiFi-GAN, MelGAN) and zero-shot voice cloning architectures (VALL-E, Tortoise-TTS, XTTS), synthesizing highly authentic human speech now requires merely 3 to 10 seconds of reference audio. This capability has precipitated severe security threats, including:

CEO / Vishing Fraud
Unauthorized voice spoofing used to authorize fraudulent financial transfers.
Biometric Bypass
Deceiving voice biometric authentication systems in telebanking.
Misinformation
Fabricating synthetic audio of public figures, officials, and celebrities.

2. Project Objectives

  • High-Resolution Feature Extraction: Convert 1D time-domain signals into 2D Log Mel-Spectrogram representations capturing harmonic nuances.
  • Deep CNN Binary Classification: Construct a 4-stage convolutional neural network capable of discerning genuine human vocal tract resonance from vocoder phase artifacts.
  • Dual Detection Interface: Provide unified forensic inspection for pre-recorded files (WAV/MP3/FLAC/OGG) and live microphone speech via Web Audio API.
  • Acoustic Transparency: Provide interactive waveform plots, Mel spectrograms, and downloadable PDF forensic audit reports.

The CNN architecture utilizes 4 hierarchical convolutional blocks with kernel size 3x3, Batch Normalization to prevent internal covariate shift, ReLU activations, Max Pooling, and Spatial Dropout2d regularization to prevent overfitting on specific speaker acoustic signatures. Fully connected linear dense layers map the feature embeddings into 2 class logits for binary classification.

Real-World Applications
  • Financial Banking Verification: Real-time protection for banking customer phone lines and wire authorization desks.
  • Media & Forensic Journalism: Verification of leaked phone recordings, voice memos, and whistleblower statements.
  • Cybersecurity Threat Intelligence: Automated screening of incoming VoIP traffic against synthesized voice injection attacks.
Future Scope
  • Self-Supervised Foundations: Integration of Wav2Vec 2.0 and Whisper encoder representations for zero-shot generalized spoof detection.
  • Streaming Window Inference: Low-latency rolling analysis for ongoing live telephone calls.
  • Cross-Lingual Benchmarking: Expansion across multilingual dialect datasets with noise-invariant augmentations.
